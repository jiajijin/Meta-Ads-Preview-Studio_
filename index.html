Paste your copy or upload a .docx, .txt, .md or .csv file. Label each piece (for example “Headline 2: …”) and it goes into the right place.

Accepted labels
Ad copy — Primary text, Headline, Description, CTA, URL. Number them for variants: Headline 1, Headline 2… (or repeat the label).
Carousel — Card 1 headline, Card 1 description, Card 2 headline…
Instant form — Form intro, Form headline, Form description, Form button, Form link.
CSV — use these names as column headers; each row becomes a variant.
Only the fields you include are changed. Bullets, numbered lists, and lines or paragraphs separated by blank lines are each read as separate items. Text without labels is sorted by length.
